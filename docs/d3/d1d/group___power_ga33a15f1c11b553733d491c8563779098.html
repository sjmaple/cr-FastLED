◆ dither_reserve_mW()

fl::u32 dither_reserve_mW ( fl::span< const CRGB > leds)

The most BINARY_DITHER can add to one latched frame of leds, in mW (#4342, contract R4: "quantization reserve").

Dither is applied before scaling and only ever raises a lit channel – dither() is b ? qadd8(b, d) : 0 – by up to two output codes at any non-zero brightness (the offset d <= 256/s is scaled back by s, and the FASTLED_SCALE8_FIXED rounding can carry once more). So the reserve is two codes on every lit channel, at the power model's steepest single-code step, rounded up. It does not scale with brightness, which is why the limiter adds it to the fixed part of the demand rather than the scaled part.
